### What is container orchestration?

---

Automated management of containerized applications across multiple hosts. It handles:

- **Deployment & scaling** of containers
- **Load balancing** and traffic routing
- **Health monitoring** and self-healing
- **Resource allocation** and optimization
- **Networking** between containers
- **Storage provisioning**

## Why it matters

Manually managing containers at scale is impractical. Orchestration platforms automate lifecycle management so you can focus on application logic.
