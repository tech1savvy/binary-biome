### What are the three database deployment models on AWS?

---

**1. On-Premises**: You handle everything (power, cooling, hardware, OS, DB install, patching, backups). **2. Unmanaged (DB on EC2)**: AWS handles physical infrastructure; you handle DB install, patching, HA, backups. **3. Managed (Amazon RDS)**: AWS handles provisioning, patching, backups, and HA; you focus on app tuning, schema, and access control.
