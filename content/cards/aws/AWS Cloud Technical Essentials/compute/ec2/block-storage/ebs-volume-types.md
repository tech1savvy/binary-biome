### What are the EBS volume types?

---

| Type | Category | Use Case | Max IOPS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provisioned IOPS | SSD | Latency-sensitive transactional DBs | 64,000 |
| General Purpose | SSD | Boot volumes, dev/test, interactive apps | 16,000 |
| Throughput Optimized | HDD | Big data, log processing, data warehouses | 500 |
| Cold HDD | HDD | Less frequently accessed workloads | 250 |
