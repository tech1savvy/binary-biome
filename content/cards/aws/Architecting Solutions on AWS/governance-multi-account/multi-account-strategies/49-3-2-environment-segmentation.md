### 2. Environment Segmentation

---

Different account types encourage both experimentation and security.

**Sandbox Accounts**

- isolated experimentation environments
- minimal restrictions
- not connected to enterprise data.

**Development Accounts**

- used for normal development work
- access to approved services
- limited internal data access.

**Test / Production Accounts**

- tightly controlled
- strong security and governance
- used for final workloads.

Typical environment pipeline: _Sandbox → Dev → Test → Production_
