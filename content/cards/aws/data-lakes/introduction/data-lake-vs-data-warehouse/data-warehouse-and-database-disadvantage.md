# Why not just store everything in a database or data warehouse?

---

- Databases and data warehouses introduce barriers — you have to go through structured schemas and processes to get information out. Data lakes remove those barriers so you can move quickly.

---

- Data lakes enable answers in days, hours, or real time. Traditional databases can't match that velocity at scale.
