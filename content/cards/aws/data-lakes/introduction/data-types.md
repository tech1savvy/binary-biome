###### What are the common data types in a data lake?

---

- **Raw** — original, unmodified format (e.g. log files, raw sensor data)
- **Formatted** — optimized form (column/index data for discovery and research)
- **Transformed** — business rules applied, cleaned, ready for deeper analytics
- **Published** — closely governed, managed datasets ready for broad use and visualization dashboards
