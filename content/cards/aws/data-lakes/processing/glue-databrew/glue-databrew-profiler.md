# What does the AWS Glue DataBrew profiler do?

---

- Analyzes the full dataset and recommends data-shaping actions
- Detects duplicates and PII (credit cards, bank accounts, driver's licenses, names, addresses, DOBs)
- Reports stats: % empty cells, duplicate count, classified PII fields
