###### What are the suggested IAM roles for Lake Formation?

---

- **IAM admin** — manages all of IAM
- **Data lake administrator** — full control over all data
- **Data engineer** — write/manage ETL and processing
- **Data analyst** — read-only access to curated datasets
- **Workflow role** — assumed by other AWS services to act on your behalf
