What is the purpose of the `cors` middleware in Express?

---

- It is a middleware that simplifies handling Cross-Origin Resource Sharing (CORS).
- It enables a server to indicate to a browser which origins are permitted to access its resources.
- It sets the necessary `Access-Control-*` HTTP headers on the response.

---

- It is a separate NPM package and has not been bundled with Express since Express 4.x.
