What is the ECMA-262 specification?

---

- Contains the most in-depth, detailed, and formalized information about JavaScript.
- Defines the JavaScript language.
- It is a trustworthy source for language details but difficult to understand for everyday use.

---

- A new specification version is released every year.
- Latest draft: https://tc39.es/ecma262/
- Proposals for new bleeding-edge features (Stage 3): https://github.com/tc39/proposals
- Other specifications exist for browser development.
