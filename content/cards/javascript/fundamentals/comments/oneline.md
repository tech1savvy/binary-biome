Which option to use for single-line comments in JavaScript?

---

- Use two forward slashes `//`.
- All text from `//` to the end of the line is treated as a comment and ignored by the JavaScript engine.

---

- Single-line comments can occupy a full line or follow a statement.

```javascript
// This comment occupies a line of its own
alert('Hello'); // This comment follows the statement
```
