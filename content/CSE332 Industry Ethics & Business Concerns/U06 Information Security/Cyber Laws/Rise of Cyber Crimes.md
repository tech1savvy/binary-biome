- Cybercrime has been increasing at an alarming rate worldwide.
- With the rapid growth of digital transactions, cloud computing, and social media, cybercriminals have found new ways to exploit individuals, businesses, and governments.
- The rise in cybercrime can be attributed to greater internet penetration, lack of cybersecurity awareness, and evolving hacking techniques.

# Statistics and Reports
- Cybercrime is expected to cost the world $10.5 trillion annually by 2025 (Cybersecurity Ventures report).
- A cyberattack occurs every 39 seconds worldwide.
- In 2023, more than 800 million ransomware attacks were reported globally.
- Over 50,000 cybercrime cases were registered in India in 2023 (NCRB Data).

# Major Reasons Behind the Rise in Cyber Crime
- Increased Digital Dependence
- Lack of Cyber Security Awareness
- Advanced Hacking Techniques
- Rise of Mobile & IoT Devices  
  *(43% of cyber-attacks are now directed at mobile devices.)*
- Financial Motivation
