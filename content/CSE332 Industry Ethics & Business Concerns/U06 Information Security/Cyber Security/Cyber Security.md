# Best Practices
- *Regular* software *updates and patch* management
- Implement *Multi-Factor Authentication* (MFA)
- Conduct employee *cybersecurity awareness* *training*
- Use *strong*, unique *passwords* and a *password manager*
- *Backup* critical *data* regularly and securely
- Utilize *firewalls*, *antivirus*, and *endpoint protection*
- Perform regular *vulnerability assessments* and *penetration testing*
- Enforce *least privilege access controls*
- Deploy *network segmentation* and monitoring tools
- Establish a robust *incident response and recovery plan*

# Emergin Trends
- **AI-powered threat detection** offers predictive analytics. 
- **Secure Access Service Edge** (SASE) enhances network security. 
- **Password less authentication** uses biometrics. 
- **Cloud**-focused tools protect multi-cloud environments.

# Keynote
- <u>Cybersecurity is continuous</u>, not a one-time fix. Combine tools, processes, and trained people. 
- Adopt new tools, upgrade existing systems, and educate your team to stay ahead.
