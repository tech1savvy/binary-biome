# Case Study 1: Colonial Pipeline Ransomware Attack (2021)
- The Colonial **Pipeline attack** disrupted the U.S. fuel supply.
- Hackers received a $4.4 million Bitcoin payment.
- This case highlights the importance of:
	- *Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)*
	- *Network segmentation*

# Case Study 2: Email Phishing Against a Fortune 500 Company
- A **phishing attack** led to a $100 million loss in wire fraud.
- The attack vector involved deceptive emails impersonating executives.
- To prevent email scams, implement:
	- *Staff training*
	- *SPF, DKIM, and DMARC email authentication protocols*

# Case Study 3: Data Breach at Equifax (2017)
- The Equifax **data breach** compromised personal data of 147 million people.
- Cause: An unpatched vulnerability in Apache Struts software.
- This case illustrates the critical need for:
	- *Timely software updates*
	- *Effective patch management*
