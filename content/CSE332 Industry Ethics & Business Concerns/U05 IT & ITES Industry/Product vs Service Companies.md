# Product-Based IT Companies
## Examples
- Microsoft
- Google
- Apple
- Adobe
- Sony
## What they do?
- They *create and sell products* like
	- software :Windows, Microsoft Office
	- and hardware: Surface Laptops, XBOX.
## How They Make Money?
- By selling their products, either as one-time purchases i.e. *licences* or through *subscriptions* like (Office 365).

# Service-Based IT Companies
## Examples
- TCS
- Infosys
- Accenture
## What they do?
- They provide services like  
	- consulting, 
	- IT solutions & support
- i.e. helping companies improve their technology.
	- eg, cloud services or custom software development.
## How They Make Money?
- By charging clients for services, often based on the *project* or ongoing *contracts*.
## Advantages
- Long-Term Relationships: They work closely with clients and can get *repeat business*.
- Tailored Solutions: They customise services to meet the client's specific needs.
## Challenges
- They rely heavily on skilled workers and need to manage costs carefully.

# Differences

- > You might be wondering, Microsoft & Google also offer some services through their products? Yes, but their core selling are products. Where as companies like TCS & Infosys do not have their own products.

- **Product Based Companies** do *higher R&D Investments* *than* Service Based Companies.
- **Service based companies** have *clients* whereas,
  **Product based companies** have *customers*.
