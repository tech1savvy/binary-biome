- The **tech industry** is still *struggling with diversity*.

# Gender Diversity
- **Women** make up only *28%* of all **tech jobs**.

# Ethnic Diversity
- **White Americans** **hold** most tech jobs about *62%* , whereas minorities like *Black and Hispanic* are still **underrepresented** in tech, especially in *leadership roles*.

# Efforts to Improve
- Gender and Ethnic Diverse hiring policies.
