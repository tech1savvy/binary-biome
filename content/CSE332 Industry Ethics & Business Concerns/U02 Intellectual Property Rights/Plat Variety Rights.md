- *Plant Variety Rights (PVRs)* protect breeders from unauthorized reproduction, production, marketing, selling, importing, and exporting of their plant varieties.

- Examples: *Pusa Basmati 1121* | [source::internet]
    - <u>Origin</u>: Developed by the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI)
    - <u>Description:</u> A basmati rice variety known for its extra-long grain and aromatic fragrance, widely cultivated in northern India.
    - <u>Recognition</u>: Protected under the PPV&FR Act, 2001.
