- **Legal Ownership** – Registration is prima facie evidence of proprietorship.
- **Exclusive Rights** – Allows exclusive use in connection with goods/services.
- **Legal Protection** – Enables legal action against infringement.
- **Transferability** – The proprietor can assign or license the trademark like any other property.
- **Goodwill & Perpetual Ownership** – As long as the trademark is renewed periodically, the owner can continue to enjoy its goodwill indefinitely.
