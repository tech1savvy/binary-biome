- Protects the three-dimensional shape or configuration of a product or its packaging.
- Granted when the shape itself is distinctive and serves as a source identifier.
- Considered a non-traditional trademark as it extends beyond text or logos.

- **Example**: Shape of *Coco-Cola Glass Bottle*
