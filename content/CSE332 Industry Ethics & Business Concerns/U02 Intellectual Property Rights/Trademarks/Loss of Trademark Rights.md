- **Improper use** – Incorrect usage may lead to loss of distinctiveness.
- **Failure to police** – Not taking action against infringement weakens rights.
- > **Genericide** – When a *trademark becomes* a *common term* (e.g., Xerox, Aspirin).
- **Failure to renew** – Non-renewal leads to expiration of rights.
- **Non-use** – Prolonged non-use may result in cancellation.
- **Cancellation** – Authorities can cancel a trademark if it does not meet requirements.
- **Improper assignment or licensing** – Unauthorized transfers may lead to loss of ownership.
