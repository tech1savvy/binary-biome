- Identifies *products or goods* instead of services.
- The product is the company's unique selling feature.
- Uses TM (™) initially to indicate an unregistered mark.
- Once registered, the *® symbol* is used.
- **Examples:** Pepsi®, Maggi®, PHILIPS®.
