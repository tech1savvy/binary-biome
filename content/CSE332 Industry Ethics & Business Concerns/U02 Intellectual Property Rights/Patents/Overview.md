- > A **patent** is a legal document that gives the inventor *exclusive rights to* their *invention* for a *set period*.
- > It *protects* the *invention* from being made, *used*, or sold *without* the *inventor's permission*.
___
- **Patent** – A *statutory grant* by the government for new inventions.
- **Monopoly Rights** – Grants *exclusive rights* to the inventor for a defined period.
- **Disclosure Requirement** – The inventor must *fully disclose* the *invention*.
- Legal Protection – Prevents others from using, making, or selling the invention without permission.
- **Territorial Rights** – Patent rights apply only *within the country* where they are granted.
- **Term** – Valid for *20 years* from the date of filing.
