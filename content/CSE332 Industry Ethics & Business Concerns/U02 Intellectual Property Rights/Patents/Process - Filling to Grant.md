# Filing of Application
- The patent application can be **filed as** a *provisional or complete* specification.
- If a provisional specification is filed, the complete specification must be filed within 12 months.

# Publication of Application
- The patent application is **published** *after 18 months* from the priority date unless early publication is requested.

# Request for Examination (RFE)
- The applicant must file an **examination request** *within 48 months* from the priority date or filing date, whichever is earlier.
- For a **divisional application**, the RFE must be filed *within 6 months* from the filing of the further application.

# Examination & FER (First Examination Report)
- The Controller refers the application to an Examiner within 1 month of publication or receipt of RFE.
- The Examiner prepares the examination report within 1-3 months.
- The Controller disposes of the Examiner's report within 1 month of receipt.
- The First Examination Report (FER) is **issued** *within 6 months* of the RFE or publication, whichever is later.
- The applicant must **comply with all objections** *within 12 months*.

# Pre-Grant Opposition
- *Third parties* may *file* a pre-grant opposition *after publication* **but** *before the grant* of the patent.

# Grant of Patent
- If all objections are cleared, the patent is granted.

# Post-Grant Opposition
- A post-grant opposition can be filed by any interested party *within 12 months* of the grant.
- The Controller reviews the opposition and makes a decision.
- If the opposition is rejected, the patent remains valid.
- If the opposition is accepted, the patent may be revoked or amended.

# Appeals and Revocation
- If **dissatisfied with the Controller**’s decision, the party can *appeal* to the *Appellate Board*.
- **Further** appeals can be made to the *High Court*.
- If the High Court revokes the patent, it is no longer valid.
