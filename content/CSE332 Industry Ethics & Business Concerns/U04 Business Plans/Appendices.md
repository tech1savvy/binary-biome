The appendices **contain** *letters, charts*, and *other* important *data* *supporting* the business *plan*.

## Include:
- *Brochures* and advertising materials
- Industry studies
- *Blueprints* and plans
- Maps and photos of location
- Magazine or other articles
- *Equipment lists* (owned or to be purchased)
- *Leases* and *contracts*
- Letters of support from future customers
- Market research studies
- List of assets available as loan collateral
