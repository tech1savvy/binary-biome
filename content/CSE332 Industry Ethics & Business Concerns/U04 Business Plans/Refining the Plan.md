The generic business plan should be customised for your business type and target audience.

# For Raising Capital
## For Bankers:
- **Amount of Loan**: Clearly state the loan amount.
- **How Funds Will Be Used**: Explain fund allocation.
- **What This Will Accomplish**: Describe business impact.
- **Requested Repayment Terms**: Provide schedule.
- **Collateral Offered**: List assets offered as security.
- **Existing Liens**: Mention any current liens on assets.

## For Investors:
- **Funds Needed Short-Term**: Immediate capital needs.
- **Funds Needed in 2–5 Years**: For scaling and expansion.
- **How Funds Will Be Used**: Product, marketing, operations.
- **Estimated ROI**: Project investor returns.
- **Exit Strategy**: IPO, acquisition, or buyback.
	- <u>IPO</u>: *Initial Public Offering*
		- An IPO is when a *private company* *offers* its *shares* *to* the *public* for the *first time on a stock exchange*. It marks the transition from a private company to a public company.
	- *<u>Buyback</u>*: 
		- A buyback is when a company *purchases* its *own shares* *from* the *stock market*. This reduces the number of shares available to the public.
- **Investor Involvement**: Equity, advisory roles.

# For Type of Business
## Manufacturing
- Planned production levels
- Cost comparison with industry
- Product line pricing
- Gross profit margins
- Production capacity constraints
- Inventory and new product plans
## Service Businesses
- Key competitive factors
- Pricing methods
- Production management
- Quality control procedures
- Labor productivity tracking
- Subcontracting, credit, and client strategy
