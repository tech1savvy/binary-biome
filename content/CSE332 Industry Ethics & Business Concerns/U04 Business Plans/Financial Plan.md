Financial planning is the task of determining *how* a business will *afford to achieve* its strategic *goals* and objectives.
- **12 Month Profit & Loss Statement**: Project profitability, determine path to success.
- **Three-Year Profit Projection (Optional)**: Long-term growth planning.
- **Projected Cash Flow**: Plan for cash needs, preliminary expenses, and reserves.
- **For Each Item**: Track expected cash receipt timing and expense payment schedules.
- **Opening Day Balance Sheet**: Fundamental *financial report* *for* *launch* readiness.
- **Break-Even Analysis**: Determine sales level required to cover costs in case of bankruptcy.
