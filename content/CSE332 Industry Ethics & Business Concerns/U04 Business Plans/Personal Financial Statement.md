A personal financial statement is a document or spreadsheet *outlining* an *individual's financial position* at a given point in time.
___
# Benefits
- Personal financial statements help track spending and increase  in net worth.
# Types
- Two primary types of financial statements:
  - **Personal Cash Flow Statement**: Tracks money *earned* and *spent*, *showing* whether you have a positive or negative *cash flow*.
  - **Personal Balance Sheet**: Summaries your *assets* and *liabilities* to *calculate* your *net worth*.
# Key Points
- *Assets and liabilities outside business*.
- Personal net worth for each owner.
- Key for business financing clarity.
- Owners may use personal assets.
- Shows available assets for business.
- Investors and bankers require details.
