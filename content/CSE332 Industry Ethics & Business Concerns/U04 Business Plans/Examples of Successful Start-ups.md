# Flipkart

## History:
Launched in 2007, Flipkart began during the early days of Indian e-commerce. Though registered in Singapore, the company is headquartered in Bangalore, India.

## Founders:
- **Sachin Bansal** and **Binny Bansal**, both ex-Amazon employees and alumni of IIT Delhi, originally from Chandigarh, India.

## How it Started:
- Initially focused on selling books.
- Gradually expanded to a wide range of products.

## Funding History:
- Early funding reached ₹200 million.
- Valuation eventually grew to $15 billion.

## Evolution:
- Introduced **Cash on Delivery (COD)** and card payment on delivery, revolutionizing Indian e-commerce.
- Built an in-house delivery system for reliable service.

## Acquisitions:
- Acquired **Myntra**, **LetsBuy**, and others.
- Evolved into India’s largest e-commerce platform.


# MakeMyTrip

## Founder:
- **Deep Kalra**, founder of MakeMyTrip.com, based in Gurgaon.

## Journey:
- Started his career after earning a master’s degree from IIM Ahmedabad.
- Worked at **GE Capital** and **ABN AMRO Bank**.
- Left to join **AMF Bowling** to set up entertainment centers in India.

## Realization:
- Despite opening over 200 bowling lanes, he felt unfulfilled and underutilized under foreign management.

## Turning Point:
- Saw the internet’s potential and quit his job.
- Founded **MakeMyTrip** to simplify online travel bookings.
- Quickly became a leader in the Indian travel industry.

## Achievements:
- Chairperson of NASSCOM Internet Working Group
- Member of NASSCOM Executive Council
- Member of CII’s Tourism sub-committee
- **Awards:**
  - Best Travel Portal India – World Travel Awards (2013, 2014)
  - E-tailer of the Year – ET Retail Awards (2013, 2014)
  - Best Online Travel Service – Times Travel Honours (2011)
  - One of the Ten Best Companies to Work For in India (2010–2013)
