# Overview
- **Launch** & Objective
    - Launched on *January 16, 2016*
    - Aims to promote entrepreneurship, innovation, and job creation
    - Provides financial support, legal simplifications, and mentorship
- Example: **Benefiting startups** like *BYJU’S*, *Zomato*, and *Paytm*
- **Impact** & Future of Start-up India
	- India is the *3rd largest startup ecosystem* globally.

# Eligibility & Registration Process
The **Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade** (DPIIT) defines a startup as:

- A company *less than 10 years old* from the date of incorporation (the official date when a company is legally registered).
- Annual **turnover** *not exceeding INR 100 crore* in any financial year (a 12-month accounting period, typically from April to March in India).
- Should **not** be **formed by** *splitting* or *restructuring* an *existing business*.

# Benefits of the Startup India Scheme

1. **Tax Benefits**
    - *Income Tax Exemption*: Eligible startups can avail a *100%* tax exemption on profits *for* *three consecutive years* *within the first ten years* of incorporation.
    - *Exemption from Angel Tax*: Investments received by startups from [^1]angel investors are also exempted from taxation.  
    - *Capital Gains Tax Exemption*: Startups can claim exemptions on long-term capital gains if the amount is reinvested in a specified fund or utilized to purchase assets as per government guidelines.  

2. **Simplified Compliance & Self-Certification**
    - Startups are permitted to [^2]*self-certify compliance with nine labor and environmental laws*, reducing regulatory burdens and focusing on core business activities.  

3. **Simplified Company Registration**
    - The Startup India portal offers an online system for easy and quick company registration with minimal documentation.  

4. **Funding Support**
    - *Fund of Funds for Startups* (<u>FFS</u>): A **₹10,000 crore** fund managed by the **Small Industries Development Bank of India** (<u>SIDBI</u>) to support startups through venture capital firms.
    - *Credit Guarantee Scheme* (<u>CGS</u>): Provides *collateral-free loans* to startups, facilitating access to financial assistance.

5. **Fast-Track Patent & Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Support**
    - Startups receive an *80% rebate on patent* filing fees and a *50% rebate on trademark* filing fees. Additionally, they benefit from *fast-track examination* **of patent applications** and *free legal assistance* **for IPR protection**.

6. **Government Tenders & Procurement Benefits**
    - Startups are *exempted from* the *requirement of prior experience or turnover* to participate in government tenders, providing equal opportunities in public procurement.  

7. **Incubation & Networking Opportunities**
    - The *Startup India Hub* connects entrepreneurs with mentors, investors, and incubators. Access to *Atal Incubation Centers* (<u>AICs</u>) and startup accelerators is also facilitated to support growth and development.

8. **Easier Exit for Startups**
    - Startups can *wind up operations within 90 days* under the *Insolvency and* *<u>Bankruptcy</u>* *Code*, 2016, providing a swift and straightforward exit process.

[^1]: Angel investors are *high-net-worth individuals* who fund early-stage startups in exchange for equity or convertible debt, often providing mentorship and network access.

[^2]: Self-certify compliance means startups can *declare on their own that they are following* certain *labour* *and* *environmental* *laws* without needing immediate inspections or approvals from government authorities.
