# 1. Startup India Initiative
- **Tax Exemptions**: Eligible startups receive a 100% tax exemption on profits for *three consecutive years* *within* their *first ten years* of operation.
- **Funding Support**: Through the *Fund of Funds for Startups* (<u>FFS</u>), managed by the *Small Industries Development Bank of India* (<u>SIDBI</u>), the government contributes to various *Alternative Investment Funds* (<u>AIFs</u>) that invest in startups.
- **Simplified Regulations**: Startups benefit from *easier compliance norms*, including *self-certification* under labor and environmental laws. `This is bad in long run for labour and environment health.`


# 2. MUDRA (Micro Units Development and Refinance Agency) Loan Scheme

- Also known as Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY):
- Eligibility: Startups, MSMEs (*Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises*)
- Types of Loans:  
  - **Shishu**: Up to ₹50,000 (For new businesses)  
  - **Kishor**: ₹50,000 to ₹5 lakh (For growing businesses)  
  - **Tarun**: ₹5 lakh to ₹10 lakh (For established businesses)  
- *No collateral* required

# 3. Stand-Up India

This scheme aims to empower *SC/ST* and *women entrepreneurs* by offering:
- **Loan Amount**: Between *₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore*.
- **Eligibility**: At least *51% ownership* by SC/ST & Women

# 4. Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)

AIM promotes a culture of innovation through:
- **Atal Tinkering Labs**: Established *labs in schools to nurture curiosity* and creativity among students.
- **Atal Incubation Centers**: Set up to *support startups by* providing necessary *infrastructure* and *mentorship*.

# 5. Government e-Marketplace (GeM)

GeM facilitates startups to:
- **Sell Products/Services**: Directly to various government departments and organizations, ensuring a transparent procurement process.

# 6. Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

SISFS provides *financial assistance to early-stage startups*  upto *50 Lakh* for Proof of Concept, Prototype Development, Product Trials, Market Entry.

# 7. Credit Guarantee Fund (CGF) Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE)  
- **Loan Amount**: Up to ₹2 crore  
- **Eligibility**: Startups & MSMEs in manufacturing, service, and retail sectors  
- **Collateral**: Collateral-free for loans up to ₹2 crore

# 8. Bank-Specific Startup Loans  
- Many banks offer customized startup loans:  
  - **SBI Startup Loan** – Business loans *for MSMEs and startups*  
  - **HDFC Bank Business Growth Loan** – Unsecured loans *for startups*  
  - **ICICI Bank Business Loan** – Loans *for working capital and expansion*  
  - **Axis Bank Business Loan** – Quick approval loans *for small businesses*
