- Startups usually begin with limited resources and depend on funding from investors, venture capitalists, or crowdfunding.
- Their main **goal** is *rapid growth* and *scalability* to compete in the market.
- Most startups **start with** *small teams* and expand if they succeed.
- While some achieve great success, many struggle with challenges and risks that can lead to failure.
