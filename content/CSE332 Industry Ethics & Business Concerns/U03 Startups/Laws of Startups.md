# Dispute Resolution Laws

- > **Mediation**: A *neutral third party* helps *resolve* a *dispute* outside of court.
- > **Arbitration**: A legal process where an arbitrator (like a *private judge*) makes a binding decision.
- > **Litigation**: Taking a dispute to *court* (usually costly and slow).
- **Dispute Clauses**: Contracts should include how conflicts will be handled.
- **Legal Battles**: Try to avoid lawsuits, as they can be expensive and time-consuming.
- **Legal Consultation**: Get expert advice when handling disputes.

# Business Entity Laws

- **Business Structure**: Choose how your business is set up—Sole Proprietorship (*one-person business*), Partnership (shared ownership), LLC (Limited Liability Company, protects personal assets), or Corporation (separate legal entity).
- **Liability Protection**: Some structures (like *<u>LLCs</u> and Corporations*) *protect your personal assets* if your business gets sued.
- **Tax Implications**: Different structures have different tax rules. Some pay taxes directly, while others pass them to owners.
- **Registration**: You must register your business with the state and submit required documents (like business licenses).
- **Compliance**: Keep up with legal requirements like yearly reports and fees.
- **Legal Advice**: A lawyer can help you choose the best structure for your business.
# Employment Laws
- & Important
- **Anti-Discrimination**: You can’t refuse to hire someone based on race, gender, or religion.
- **Minimum Wage & Overtime**: Employers must pay at least the legal minimum and extra for overtime.
- **Workplace Safety**: Businesses must follow **<u>OSHA</u>** *(Occupational Safety and Health Administration)* **rules** to keep employees safe.
- **Employment Contracts**: Written agreements that explain job roles, salaries, and rules.
- **<u>NDAs</u> (Non-Disclosure Agreements)**: Legal documents preventing employees from sharing business secrets.
- **Wrongful Termination**: Firing someone unfairly can lead to lawsuits. Follow legal procedures.

# Taxation Laws

- **Income Tax**: Businesses must report and pay taxes on their earnings, either yearly or quarterly.
- **Sales Tax**: If you sell products, you must collect and send sales tax to the government.
- **Payroll Taxes**: Businesses must take out *money for employees’ benefits*, like Social Security.
- **R&D Tax Credits**: If you develop new technology or products, you might get tax breaks.
- **Financial Records**: Keep clear records to avoid trouble with tax authorities.
- **Tax Professionals**: Experts help businesses follow tax laws correctly.

# Fundraising and Securities Laws

-  **<u>SEC</u> (Securities and Exchange Commission)**: *Government agency* that *regulates* *investments* and *stock* sales.
- **Investor Disclosure**: Businesses must tell investors important facts to avoid fraud (deception).
- **Fraud Prevention**: Lying to investors is illegal and can lead to jail time.
- **Crowdfunding**: *Raising money from the public* (like **Kickstarter**) must follow legal rules.
- **Debt Financing**: Borrowing money with a clear loan agreement.
- **Legal Guidance**: Lawyers help navigate complex fundraising rules.

# Data Privacy and Cybersecurity Laws

- **<u>GDPR</u> (General Data Protection Regulation)**: A *European law* that *protects people’s online data*.
- **<u>CCPA</u> (California Consumer Privacy Act)**: A *U.S. law* that gives California residents control over their data.
- **Encryption**: A method of securing data so hackers can’t steal it.
- **Data Breach Notification**: Businesses must inform customers if their personal information is stolen.
- **User Consent**: Companies must get permission before collecting personal data.
- **Cybersecurity Audits**: Regularly check systems for security weaknesses.

# Intellectual Property (IP) Laws

- **Patents** (legal rights for inventions): Protect your inventions for 20 years, stopping others from copying them.
- **Trademarks** (brand protection): Protects your business name, logo, or slogan so others can’t use them.
- **Copyrights** (creative rights): Protect books, music, software, and other original works.
- **Trade Secrets** (confidential business info): Protects formulas, processes, or strategies that give you an advantage.
- **IP Registration**: Officially registering your IP gives you stronger legal protection.
- **IP Infringement**: If someone copies your IP without permission, you can take legal action.

# Contract Laws

- **Contracts**: Legal agreements that explain the rights and responsibilities of each party.
- **Key Clauses**: Important parts of a contract, like payment terms and what happens if something goes wrong.
- **Mutual Consent**: Both sides must agree voluntarily for a contract to be valid.
- **NDAs (Non-Disclosure Agreements)**: Prevents people from sharing private business info.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Contracts should include how problems will be solved (negotiation, mediation, or court).
- **Legal Review**: Regularly check contracts to ensure they follow laws.


# Consumer Protection Laws

- **Product Safety**: Items sold to customers must be safe to use.
- **False Advertising**: Businesses can’t lie about products in ads.
- **Refund & Return Policies**: Customers must clearly know how to return products.
- **Warranties & Guarantees**: Promises that a product will work as described.
- **Data Protection**: Companies must keep customer information safe.
- **Complaint Resolution**: Handle customer complaints quickly and fairly.
