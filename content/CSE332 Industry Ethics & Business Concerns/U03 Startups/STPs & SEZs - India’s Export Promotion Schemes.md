# STP Scheme: Digital Export Engine

- Since its **inception** in *1991* by the *Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology*, the STP scheme has transformed India's **export** landscape.

## Key Features:
- **Tax holidays** i.e. up to *10-year income tax exemption* and a streamlined *single-window clearance process*.
- *100%* <u>FDI</u> (*Foreign Direct Investment*) allowed in <u>IT/ITES</u> (IT-Enabled Services)sectors.
- Focus on *software development*, R&D, and innovation.
- *Duty-free imports* of *technology*.

# SEZ Scheme: Trade & Investment Hubs

- **Launched** in *2005* under the *SEZ Act*, Special Economic Zones have emerged as pivotal trade and investment hubs.

## Highlights:

- Over *270* operational *SEZs* as of 2023.
- Significant *tax breaks* and *GST exemptions*.
- Boost to export-oriented manufacturing and services.
- **Strategic clusters** in states like *Gujarat* and *Tamil Nadu*.

## SEZ Advantages for Exporters

- A *15-year corporate tax holiday*.
- *Duty-free import* and *export* privileges.
- An **FDI** framework that requires no additional licensing.
- *Simplified labour laws*.

## Key Differences: STP vs. SEZ
- & Important

| Feature          | STP             | SEZ                     |
| ---------------- | --------------- | ----------------------- |
| **Sector Focus** | *IT*/Software   | *Multiple sectors*      |
| **Tax Holiday**  | *10* years      | *15* years              |
| **Location**     | Dedicated parks | Larger geographic zones |
