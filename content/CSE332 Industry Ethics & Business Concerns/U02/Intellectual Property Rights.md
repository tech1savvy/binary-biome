## Intellectual Property Rights

- Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) are *legal rights*.
- > They protect and encourage the development and distribution of new products and services.
- IPRs are *granted to people over their creations*.
- Includes inventions, designs, trademarks, and creative content.

### Patent

- A **patent** is a legal document that gives the inventor *exclusive rights to* their *invention* for a *set period*.
- It *protects* the *invention* from being made, *used*, or sold *without* the *inventor's permission*.

### Copyright

- Copyright is the *right to copy*.
- *Only* the *creator* of the work *can authorize reproduction*.
- Copyright law protects creators of original material from unauthorized use.

#### **Examples of Copyrighted Works:**
- Novels
- Art
- Poetry
- Musical lyrics and compositions
- Films
- Website content

### Trademark

- A trademark is *a sign* capable of *distinguishing* goods or services.
- Trademarks are protected by intellectual property rights.
- Trademark protection can be obtained through registration.
- It grants exclusive rights to use the registered trademark.

### Trade Secret

- A trade secret is a *confidential* piece of *information*.
- **Secrecy:** The information is not generally known or accessible to competitors.
- **Commercial Value:** The information provides an economic advantage.
- **Reasonable Efforts:** Businesses take steps to keep the information secret.

- > Example: Recipe of Coco-Cola

### Geographical Indication (GI)

- A GI is a *name* or *sign* that identifies a product's *geographical origin*.
- > Used to certify that products are *made in a specific place using traditional methods*.
- Helps consumers identify quality products.
- Helps producers market their products.
- Prevents misuse of popular product names.

- **Examples**: 
	- *Bikaneri Bhujia* from Rakasthan
	- Kashmir Saffron from Jammu & Kashmir
	- Banarasi Saree from Uttar Pradesh

### Design

- Protects the *visual appearance* of a product or part of it.
- Covers shape, color, *pattern*, *texture*, and *materials*.
