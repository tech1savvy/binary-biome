# Introduction to Ethics
## What Are Ethics?

- Doing what is *fair, honest, and respectful*.
- Making good choices for yourself and others.
- Following values like trust, responsibility, and kindness.

### Key Aspects of Ethics

- A *system of moral principles*.
- Guides human behavior in society.
- *Distinguishes right* from *wrong*.
- Involves personal and professional conduct.
- Affects decision-making in various fields.
- Foundational in *establishing trust*.


## Importance of Integrity

- Following moral or ethical convictions.
- *Doing* what is *right in all circumstances*.
- Acting ethically *even when no one is watching*.
- Being true to yourself.
- > Avoiding actions that dishonor you.

## Importance of Integrity in Business World

- Builds ==trust and credibility==.
- Foundation of ethical behavior.
- Ensures long-term success.
- Enhances reputation and goodwill.
- Prevents unethical practices.
- Promotes fairness and transparency.

## What Are Business Ethics?

- Honesty.
- Avoiding conflicts.
- Compliance.
- Providing relevant information.
- Law abiding.
- Fulfilling commitments.

## Need for Business Ethics

- Survival of the business unit.
- Growth of the business unit.
- Earning goodwill.
- Improving confidence.
- Maintaining inter-relationships.
- Solving social problems.

## Improving Corporate Ethics

- *Establish* clear *ethical guidelines*.
- Lead by example (ethical leadership).
- Encourage open communication.
- Conduct regular ethics training.
- Enforce accountability measures.
- Recognize and reward ethical behavior.

## Creating an Ethical Work Environment

- Employees must have a knowledgeable resource to discuss perceived unethical practices.

# Decision Making

## Including Ethical Considerations in Decision Making (**Flow of Decision Making**)

1. Develop a *Problem Statement*
2. Identify Multiple *Alternate Solutions*
3. *Evaluate* Alternatives and *Choose* a Solution
4. *Implement* the Decision
5. *Evaluate Result*

### Approaches to Choose an alternative ethical correct:

- **Virtue Ethics Approach** – The ethical choice *best* reflects *moral virtues* in yourself and your community.
- **Utilitarian Approach** – The ethical choice which produces the greatest excess of *benefits over harm*.
- **Fairness Approach** – The ethical choice which *treats everyone equally* without favouritism or discrimination.
- **Common Good Approach** – The ethical choice which advances the *common good*. "`Sabka Bhala, Sabka Vikas`"

# Ethical Behaviour of IT Professional

## Ethics in Information Technology

Examples of ethical concerns in IT that raise public concern:

- Ethical concerns in *technology's workplace use*.
- *Copyright violation* harms music and films.
- *Spam emails* enable low-cost marketing.
- *Hacking*, *plagiarism*, and *tracking* persist.

## Profession

- > A profession is a calling that requires *specialized knowledge* and often *long and intensive academic preparation*.

### Professional Employee

- A professional is engaged in the performance of work.
- Requires specialized intellectual instruction.
- Exercises discretion and judgment.

### Characteristics of Professionals

- Doctors, lawyers, and accountants require advanced training and experience.
- They must exercise carefulness and judgment in their work.

### Are IT Workers Professionals? Yes..

- **Specialized Knowledge** – IT workers require technical expertise and problem-solving skills.
- **Ethical Behavior** – They must follow ethical guidelines in data security, privacy, and software use.
- **Accountability** – Responsible for the quality and impact of their work.
- **Commitment to Continuous Learning** – Technology evolves rapidly, requiring ongoing education and skill development.

## Common Ethical Issues for IT Users

- **Privacy Concern** – Unauthorized access to personal data and misuse of information.
- **Cybersecurity Risks** – Threats like hacking, malware, and data breaches.
- **Misuse of IT Resources** – Inappropriate use of company or public IT infrastructure.
- **Surveillance and Monitoring** – Ethical concerns regarding tracking user activity and data collection.

## Business Software Alliance (BSA)
- > Organisation to *fight Software Piracy*.
- The Business Software Alliance (**BSA**) is a trade group representing the world’s largest software and hardware manufacturers.
- Its mission is to stop the unauthorized copying of software produced by its members.
- More than 100 BSA lawyers and investigators prosecute thousands of software piracy cases each year.
- **BSA investigations** are **trigger**ed by:
    - *Calls* to the BSA *hotline*.
    - *Reports* submitted *to* the BSA *website* ([www.nopiracy.org](http://www.nopiracy.org/)).
    - *Referrals* from member companies.

## Supporting the Ethical Practices of IT Users

- **Education and Awareness** – Training users on ethical IT practices.
- **Clear Policies and Standards** – Establishing guidelines for responsible IT use.
- **Access Control and Security** – Implementing measures to protect data and systems.
- **Ethical Use of AI and Automation** – Ensuring AI-driven decisions are fair and unbiased.
- **Collaboration and Community Involvement** – Encouraging ethical discussions and teamwork.
